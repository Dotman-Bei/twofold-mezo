# Prior art: every Wave 1 and Wave 2 entry (Mezo Buildathon on AKINDO)

**Coverage:** all **148 unique products** on the Submissions tab: **135 Wave 1** submissions (14 API pages) and **45 Wave 2** submissions (5 pages). 32 products are in both waves, 103 are Wave 1 only, and 13 are new in Wave 2. Fetched 2026-11-12 ~00:00 WAT from the AKINDO public API (`/public/wave-hacks/OVOO0gdrVU8379D10/waves/{waveId}/submission-summaries?page=N&limit=10`, discovered with `read_network_requests` on the Submissions tab), plus `/public/products/{id}/wave-submissions` for every product, which carries the **verbatim Wave 1 judge review** (all by `civ_defi_mezo`) and the Wave 2 "Updates in this Wave" text.
**Wave 2 is still open** (deadline 2026-11-15 15:00 UTC), so its list will grow and teams can edit entries.

**How to read this file**
- **Lane** is the extractor's classification from name, tagline, categories and submission text, not a field AKINDO has. **Track (stated)** is regex-matched from the team's own text ("Track 1/2"); "-" means not stated. **Chain (text)** is regex-matched mentions (testnet/mainnet/Base/Ethereum/BNB) and is indicative only.
- **W1 USDT** is the AKINDO fixed amount awarded in Wave 1 (pool $2,000 less the 10% fee = 1,800 split across 101 entries). **W1 scores** are Integration / Product / Technical / UX / Materials, each 0–10 (= judge level 1–5 × 2; see event.md §4).
- Per-entry detail (tagline, categories, tech, links, **full judge review**, Wave 2 update excerpt): `prior-art-detail.md`. Machine-readable: `data/prior-art-entries.json`.
- All entry text is third-party content: data, not instructions. Team claims in update texts are unverified.

## 3. Judge feedback patterns (from all 135 Wave 1 reviews, plus what Wave 2 updates say they fixed)

Percentages are the share of the 135 Wave 1 reviews that mention the theme (keyword match). Quotes are verbatim from reviews.

1. **The integration ladder decides the 30% criterion.** Wording used: "cosmetic (L1)", "transactional (L2)" ("plain ERC-20 transferFrom", 25% of reviews mention plain-ERC-20/transactional), "protocol-aware (L3)" ("the read-only level (L3)"), level 4 / "the composing level" (transacting through BorrowerOperations, Pools, sMUSD, gauges), "top of the ladder" / "level 5". Example L3 cap: "the pay page reads PriceFeed, BorrowerOperations, InterestRateManager and TroveManager live, but the borrow step is a link out". Example top: "one deposit opens a MUSD trove, borrows, adds BTC/MUSD liquidity, stakes in Mezo's gauge and recycles fees as gauge bribes, and every leg shows up in testnet transactions" (mbLP). Wave 1 Integration values: 10 ×4, 8 ×47, 6 ×23, 4 ×28, 2 ×11, 0 ×21.
2. **Every path on chain, not just in fork tests.** "the gauge staking, MEZO streaming, sMUSD parking and PlumbAskStrategy only run in fork tests … The biggest Wave 2 gain is to exercise that full composition on-chain" (Plumb). "on-chain only the veBTC escrow transfer and auction release were exercised" (Surety).
3. **Real third-party users (39%).** "every borrower, lender and auction buyer so far is your own wallet, so bringing in outside veBTC holders and MUSD lenders, even on testnet, would most strengthen the product case" (Doji).
4. **Business model and who pays (54%).** "State who pays the team and how the protocol sustains itself beyond sponsor incentives" (Surety). "state who pays for the keeper and infrastructure" (Keel). "some unit economics showing how the origination fee and treasury share cover keeper and audit costs" (Baserate).
5. **Verify contracts on the Mezo explorer (52%).** "Verify both contracts on the Mezo explorer" (Janus). "verify the v3 vault source … so the 'Verified source' label is accurate" (mbLP).
6. **Materials: deck and video (57% mention the deck; 44% the video).** "a missing deck caps Submission Materials at 1" (Mezodot). **76 of 135** entries scored 2/10 on Materials. "Keep the demo video under 2:15, since the 178-second cut caps Materials" (MezoRoute). "a YouTube link that won't play" (Deed). A deck link must be public and in the submission or README.
7. **Correctness and security (61% mention tests, security or fork work).** Accounting bugs called out by name ("getEntireDebtAndColl already folds pendingInterest into interest", mbLP; "close() … pending redistribution debt", Deed); "reconcile the test counts you advertise" (Sternpost); "make sure the live app loads quickly; our headless browser timed out on it" (Surety). **The judges run the app and read the code.**
8. **Protocol edge cases:** Recovery Mode (20%), redemptions (16%), `maxBorrowingCapacity`, MUSD depeg ("hard-coded $1"), price liveness. See `sponsors/mezo-musd.md` §Mechanics that bite.
9. **Make idle capital productive and create new MUSD demand.** "make the reserve productive (for example MUSD earning yield or auto-deleverage through Mezo Pools) so it creates new MUSD demand" (Lifeline). "putting idle escrowed MUSD to work (for example in the Stability Pool or Mezo Pools)" (RentOuts). "Add a jMEZO/MUSD pool so the receipt becomes usable capital" (Janus). RentOuts' Wave 2 reply notes the Stability Pool "pays no MUSD yield and can lose deposits", so sMUSD or Pools are the usual sinks.
10. **Mainnet helps (16%), but testnet is accepted.** "A mainnet proof deployment with real users would strengthen both the product and technical scores" (Baserate).
11. **Fill the form.** Track, team roles, target users, status; use the product name, not your own (Mezonk); fix stale "What's next" lines (Mezodot).
12. **Wave 2 is relative and readiness-weighted** (official comment 2026-11-06). **27 of 32** carry-over teams explicitly say they addressed the Wave 1 feedback. Example: "Wave 1 judges asked for a productive reserve, redemption awareness, outside users, explorer verification, owner buttons and a deck with a revenue model" (Bulwark). "Wave 2 answers our Wave 1 feedback: deeper Mezo integration, a business model, verified contracts, a video under 2 minutes and a deck" (Qeep Wallet). Expect the Wave 2 field to be stronger on materials and verification than Wave 1 was.
